## Is IPTV legal in Canada?

“IPTV” itself is simply a method of delivering television over an internet connection. The copyright question depends on whether the provider has authorization to distribute the content it offers.

The Canadian government explains that copyright infringement can include communicating protected content to the public without authorization. Whether a particular service is authorized for particular content is a separate question that cannot be determined merely from the fact that it uses IPTV technology.

For anyone researching **iptv canada**, it is therefore worth checking the provider's content rights and the channels available to you, rather than assuming that a large channel list automatically means every stream is authorized.
